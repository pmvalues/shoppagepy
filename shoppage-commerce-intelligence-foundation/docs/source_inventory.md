# Source inventory and authority order

## Organisations

1. **CIPZ / Ministry of Justice:** controls formal legal-business registration evidence. Access and reuse terms must be confirmed; registry presence does not prove current trading activity.
2. **Sector regulators and official lists:** RBZ, POTRAZ, ZSE/VFEX, IPEC, MCAZ, ZERA, tourism and other competent authorities. Strong for licensed/regulated status within their scope.
3. **ZIMSTAT classifications and surveys:** authoritative statistical framing, not a merchant directory.
4. **Market/mall/association and partner rosters:** strong membership and location evidence when agreements define refresh and use rights.
5. **Merchant claims and field verification:** strongest source for current operating details, catalogue, branch, contact and availability when evidence/audit controls are present.
6. **Public websites, directories, maps and social pages:** discovery sources only. They require duplicate resolution, rights review and confirmation before sensitive or high-impact claims become trusted.

## Products

1. Merchant/distributor/manufacturer feeds and verified catalogues.
2. GTIN/MPN identifiers and rights-compliant brand data.
3. CPC 3.0 for universal goods/services classification; GS1 GPC for retail grouping; UNSPSC for procurement; Shoppage taxonomy for local navigation and conversion.
4. Category-expert attribute templates and compatibility rules.
5. Public listings as discovery evidence, never an unrestricted source of proprietary descriptions/images.
6. Open Food Facts full export as an ODbL global food reference source. It is useful for GTIN, taxonomy and feature enrichment, but does not establish Zimbabwe availability. Images are excluded from the current build.

## Places

1. ZIMSTAT administrative boundaries and official classifications.
2. Local-authority, market-operator and partner boundary/roster evidence.
3. Field verification for commercial clusters, buildings, stalls, access and hours.
4. GeoNames and OpenStreetMap for discovery and aliases under their licences, reconciled before production authority.

## Source precedence rule

No universal “best source” exists. Authority is predicate-specific. CIPZ may control legal name; a merchant may control current trading name and catalogue; a field visit may control observed location; a customer report may challenge stock; a regulator may control licensed status. Conflicts remain visible as claims until resolved.
