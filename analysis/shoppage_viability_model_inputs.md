# Shoppage Zimbabwe viability model inputs

## Purpose

This is a sensitivity model for a controlled pilot, not a forecast or an observed operating result.

## Sourced market inputs

- Zimbabwe Economic Census: 204,798 operational establishments; 76.1% informal; 87.9% micro; wholesale and retail trade is 73.13% of establishments.
- POTRAZ Q2 2025: 12,496,100 mobile internet subscriptions and 81.83% internet penetration.

## Derived market ceiling

- Retail-establishment ceiling = 149,777 establishments, using the exact retail count in the ZIMSTAT Economic Census presentation. The published 73.13% share is rounded, so multiplying it by 204,798 is only an approximate cross-check.
- Illustrative annual subscription revenue = potential payers * USD19 * 12.
- This is a gross ceiling only. It is not a forecast and does not assert that establishments are active, digitally ready, reachable, or willing to pay.

## Pilot sensitivity assumptions

- Growth Pilot monthly price: USD19, matching the current Shoppage operating master hypothesis.
- Software contribution margin: 85%. This is an assumption pending observed hosting, payment, support, and service-delivery costs.
- Monthly contribution per payer = USD19 * 85% = USD16.15.
- Break-even payers = monthly fixed operating cost / USD16.15, rounded up.
- Merchant activation-cost cases: USD30, USD50, and USD70.
- CAC payback months = activation cost / USD16.15.
- Fixed-cost cases: USD5,000 (lean), USD10,000 (disciplined field team), and USD15,000 (field-heavy). These are planning cases, not wage evidence.

## Calculated sensitivity outputs

| Planning case | Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lean break-even | ceiling(USD5,000 / USD16.15) | 310 paying merchants |
| Disciplined-team break-even | ceiling(USD10,000 / USD16.15) | 620 paying merchants |
| Field-heavy break-even | ceiling(USD15,000 / USD16.15) | 929 paying merchants |
| USD30 activation-cost payback | USD30 / USD16.15 | 1.86 months |
| USD50 activation-cost payback | USD50 / USD16.15 | 3.10 months |
| USD70 activation-cost payback | USD70 / USD16.15 | 4.33 months |

These outputs are arithmetic consequences of the stated assumptions only. They are not evidence that Shoppage can acquire, retain, or charge those numbers of merchants.

## Interpretation rule

No national scale decision is permitted from this model. The operating pilot must measure fully loaded merchant activation cost, paid conversion, retention, offer freshness, buyer resolution, and attributable merchant gross profit before an expansion forecast is considered valid.
