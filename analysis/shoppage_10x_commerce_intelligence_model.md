# Shoppage 10x commerce-intelligence model

## Decision

Do not replace Shoppage's five domains. Re-sequence them around one commercial spine:

> **Shoppage is a governed local-commerce intelligence network. It turns permissioned merchant truth, buyer intent, physical-market context, content and attributable outcomes into tools that help merchants, agencies, brands, markets and buyers make better local decisions.**

The public product remains **Sell, Discover, Grow**. The commercial engine becomes **capture reliable signals -> resolve commerce needs -> measure outcomes -> sell decision products and local media**.

The early merchant subscription is optional, not the primary proof point. It must not determine whether the intelligence model is pursued.

## What changes 10x

1. **From records to evidence-bearing signals.** A directory name, master product or map pin is never treated as commerce truth. Every current claim carries source, rights, timestamp, confidence, owner, review state and correction path.
2. **From a national catalogue to productised coverage cells.** The commercially useful unit is `category x geography x fulfilment mode`, not an undifferentiated count of products or companies. Each cell shows supply depth, freshness, buyer demand, response, trust and outcome evidence.
3. **From passive search to demand capture.** Search, saved needs, requests, calls, quotes, reservations, collections and fulfilment evidence all become an event stream. Unresolved demand is a paid merchant-acquisition and agency-planning signal.
4. **From ads as promotion to ads as measurement.** Ads, creator work and QR distribution must return attributable local actions, response quality and outcome confidence. They are experiments that improve the graph, not a separate vanity-media business.
5. **From a seller dashboard to decision workspaces.** Merchants act on freshness and leads; agencies plan campaigns and supply activation; brands see consented distribution/availability; markets manage rosters and category gaps.
6. **From data resale to governed intelligence.** Shoppage sells reports, decision tools, campaign measurement and privacy-safe aggregates. It does not sell buyer personal data or expose one merchant's confidential performance to another.

## The intelligence products to build

### 1. Agency Commerce Intelligence Workspace

For an agency planning a client campaign, show only permissioned or aggregate information:

- demand by category, geography and intent type;
- supply and freshness readiness by coverage cell;
- merchant/creator/market activation opportunities;
- campaign planning constraints, audience-safe reach and measurement plan;
- attributable local actions, merchant response and outcome confidence;
- weekly insight memo with a human analyst during the design-partner phase.

Start as a paid design-partner service, not self-service SaaS. The first proof is whether agencies return with another client brief, not whether they accept a dashboard login.

### 2. Brand and Distributor Availability Intelligence

- consented stockist and branch map;
- product/variant availability, price-date and stock-confidence trend;
- uncovered demand and stock-out signals;
- campaign-to-availability matching so promotion is never sent to stale or unavailable supply;
- privacy-safe category and geography benchmarks, never a competitor's confidential ledger.

### 3. Market Digital Twin

- verified operator, boundary, merchant roster, category mix and opening information;
- current offers, events, collection options and commercial content;
- footfall proxies only when a lawful, transparent measurement method exists;
- merchant-gap, request and campaign-performance reporting for the operator.

### 4. Merchant Improvement Copilot

- daily action queue for freshness, missing attributes, unanswered requests and unsafe campaigns;
- category/area benchmark shown only when cohort and confidentiality rules permit;
- recommendation explanations linked to observed evidence, not model confidence alone.

### 5. Privacy-safe Commerce Observatory

After enough strong coverage cells exist, publish or license aggregate reports on demand gaps, availability, price movement, market digitisation and local-value outcomes. This is the institutional and research product; it is not a backdoor for personal-data surveillance or political profiling.

No stage grants a client raw buyer personal data or another merchant's confidential performance. Every data product has a rights class, audience, aggregation rule, retention limit and audit log.

## The minimum data contract

Each production signal must answer: who supplied it, what exactly it claims, when it was observed, whether it may be displayed/aggregated/modelled, how it can be corrected or removed, and which decision can use it.

The first event spine is:

`source -> entity/offer/market -> observation -> verification/review -> buyer demand -> merchant response -> attributable outcome -> aggregate insight`

The critical data entities already listed in the master remain correct. Add explicit fields for rights class, display permission, aggregation permission, model-training permission, confidence, valid-through, correction state, merchant consent, client-access scope and deletion-propagation status.

## First 90 days: improve the model without expanding its scope

1. Keep the Harare Home, Power and Building wedge, but define three priority coverage cells with a named decision buyer for each.
2. Build the evidence/rights ledger, Merchant Truth OS, offer freshness, Request Network, coverage-cell engine and outcome-event instrumentation first.
3. Run three paid intelligence design-partner pilots: one agency, one brand/distributor and one market/operator. Each receives a defined question, data-access terms, baseline, deliverable and repeat-purchase test.
4. Use creator and field work as rights-cleared data acquisition with accepted-work quality metrics. Do not pay performance commissions before attribution passes.
5. Sell managed campaigns only where the cell is usable; use them to validate attribution and merchant response, not to claim broad ad reach.
6. Hold Pemosites, general subscriptions, national expansion, public APIs, marketplace checkout depth, owned fulfilment and behavioural personalisation behind the existing gates.

## KPI framework

Retain **Resolved Local Commerce Needs** as the buyer outcome. Add a separate intelligence scorecard; do not substitute one for the other.

| KPI class | Metric | Definition | Decision it governs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truth coverage | Usable/strong coverage-cell share | Priority cells meeting their defined merchant, fresh-offer and response thresholds | Whether a category/area can be advertised or sold as intelligence |
| Signal quality | Freshness, correction SLA, rights completeness and outcome-confidence rate | Share of displayed/used signals within their applicable truth, rights and audit rules | Whether a data product is safe to deliver |
| Demand value | Resolved needs and unresolved high-intent needs | Buyer needs reaching a credible outcome or remaining unserved after routing | Merchant acquisition, market expansion and agency planning |
| Commercial proof | Repeat paid intelligence engagements | Paying design partners that commission a second comparable decision product | Whether agencies/brands are a validated revenue line |
| Media proof | Cost per qualified local action and merchant response quality | Attributable action adjusted for response and outcome confidence | Whether ads/creator packages add value rather than noise |
| Guardrails | Privacy/rights incidents, complaint rate, external-platform concentration and merchant confidentiality breaches | Harm or dependency indicators | Whether to pause a source, product, client access or growth channel |

## Non-negotiable gates

- A coverage cell cannot be sold as authoritative from directory, reference-catalogue or inferred data alone.
- No agency/brand product may disclose personal data, individual merchant confidential data, or data outside its documented rights/access scope.
- The first agency/brand buyer must pay for a decision outcome, not for unproven data volume.
- Ads and creator work may not override organic relevance, freshness, disclosure, consent or trust controls.
- A model may rank or draft; it may not manufacture stock, price, trading status, verification or market containment.
- An external platform may distribute Shoppage data, but it cannot be the owner of Shoppage identity, demand, content or outcome history.

## What would falsify the strategy

The model should be narrowed or redesigned if paid design partners will not renew after receiving a credible, rights-safe decision product; if data collection cannot produce fresh usable cells at sustainable cost; if buyer requests do not reveal decision-useful demand; if merchants refuse permissioned data contribution despite clear value; or if legal/privacy review prevents the intended aggregate intelligence products.

That is a stronger test than early subscription revenue because it validates the real proposed buyer: the organisation that pays to make better local commercial decisions.
